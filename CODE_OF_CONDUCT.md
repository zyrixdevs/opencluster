# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenCluster welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We aim to make participation respectful and constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful in discussion and review.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words or actions.
- Give specific, useful feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information without consent.
- Keep project discussions focused on the work and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language or conduct, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action in response to conduct that harms the community. Reports should be sent privately to the project maintainers through the contact route listed in the repository's GitHub organization or repository settings. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially and fairly, and should not act as the sole decision-maker when they are directly involved in a report.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing OpenCluster. It is intended to guide community participation and does not replace applicable law or platform policies.
